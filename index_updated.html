Getting Around Taniti

Easy, affordable options from airport pickup to island-wide travel

Arriving in Taniti

Getting to the Island

Almost all visitors arrive by air. A small cruise ship also docks at Yellow Leaf Bay harbour once per week for one night. Taniti's airport currently serves small jets and propeller planes — airport expansion to accommodate larger jets is underway.

Arriving by cruise ship? A small cruise ship docks at Yellow Leaf Bay harbour one night per week. Taxis are available in Taniti City and most attractions are easily reachable by public bus or on foot from the harbour area.

Island Transportation Options

Car Rentals
Explore at your own pace. Rental cars from the agency near the airport. Best for families with luggage or heading island-wide.
Cars from $45/day · Scooters $25/day · Bikes $15/day
Public & Private Buses
Public buses serve Taniti City from 5 AM–11 PM daily. Private buses serve the rest of the island beyond the city limits — ideal for reaching rainforest trailheads or volcano viewpoints.
$2 per ride · $15 weekly pass
Taxis (Taniti City)
Taxis are available within Taniti City. Professional drivers with fixed rates to popular destinations and the airport. Not available island-wide — use private buses or a rental car for areas outside the city.
Base $5 · $2/mile · Airport: $25
Walking & Biking
Taniti City is flat and very walkable. The Merriton Landing area is also easy to explore entirely on foot. Bikes and helmets available from several vendors island-wide (helmets required by law).
Bike rental: $15/day · Helmets included

Before You Arrive

Essential Information

Power Outlets
120V — same as the United States. No adapter needed for US travelers.
Currency
US Dollar is the primary currency. Euros and Yen also accepted at many businesses. Major credit cards widely accepted. Several banks on the island facilitate currency exchange.
Language
Many younger Tanitians speak fluent English. However, very little English is spoken in rural areas, especially by older residents. The hospital has many multilingual employees.
Safety & Health
Violent crime is very rare. Petty theft increasing with tourism — keep valuables secure. One hospital + several clinics on-island.
Alcohol Rules
Drinking age is 18. Alcohol cannot be sold between midnight and 9:00 AM. Many tourist attractions close on national holidays.
Grocery & Convenience Stores
Taniti has two supermarkets, two smaller grocery stores, and one convenience store open 24 hours a day — so you can stock up on snacks and essentials any time.
Entertainment & Cinema
Beyond beaches and nature, Taniti offers a movie theatre, bowling alley, arcade, dance club, art galleries, and pubs including a local microbrewery — mostly concentrated in Merriton Landing.
National Holidays
Taniti has many national holidays. Tourist attractions and restaurants may close — plan accordingly and check local calendars before booking activities.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about transportation, lodging, money, safety, and island rules

Visitor Help

Plan With Confidence

These answers cover the practical questions visitors ask most often.

How do I get from the airport to my hotel?
Most hotels offer a free shuttle if arranged when booking. Taxis cost about $25 to Taniti City or Merriton Landing, and rental cars start around $45 per day.
Do taxis travel everywhere on the island?
No. Taxis operate within Taniti City. For destinations outside the city, use a private bus, rental car, bike, or a guided tour depending on the distance.
What currency should I bring?
The US dollar is the main currency. Euros and Yen are accepted at many businesses, major credit cards are widely accepted, and several banks provide currency exchange.
Is lodging inspected?
Yes. All lodging on Taniti, from hostels to the four-star resort, is regulated and regularly inspected by the Tanitian government.
What language is spoken?
Many younger Tanitians speak fluent English. English is less common in rural areas, especially among older residents. The hospital has many multilingual employees.
Are there alcohol restrictions?
The drinking age is 18. Alcohol cannot be sold between midnight and 9:00 AM.
Do attractions close on holidays?
Some tourist attractions and restaurants may close on national holidays. Check local schedules before booking activities on a holiday.
Where can I buy groceries and essentials?
Taniti has two supermarkets, two smaller grocery stores, and one convenience store that is open 24 hours a day.
What happens after I submit a booking?
The prototype confirms the request on screen and provides a booking reference. A confirmation email with the payment link and full booking details is then sent to the email address entered in the form.
Taniti
A small tropical island in the Pacific with an indigenous population of about 20,000. Less than 500 sq miles of varied terrain — sandy and rocky beaches, a safe harbour, lush rainforest, and an active volcano. Until a recent increase in tourism, most of the Tanitian economy was dominated by fishing and agriculture.
